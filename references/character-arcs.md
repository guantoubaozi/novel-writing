# Character Arcs

## Define the arc

Record each element separately:

- **External desire:** Name the observable outcome the character pursues.
- **Internal need:** Name the truth, capacity, or change required for durable resolution.
- **Contradiction:** Pair the character's self-image or claim with behavior that undermines it.
- **Wound:** Identify the past harm shaping current interpretation without using it as automatic destiny.
- **Secret:** State who knows it, who suspects it, and what disclosure would cost.
- **Boundary:** Define what the character refuses to do and what pressure could test that refusal.
- **Agency:** Track decisions that cause story change, including costly refusal.

## Stage transformation

1. Show the initial strategy succeeding enough to remain credible.
2. Increase pressure until that strategy harms the pursuit or a valued relationship.
3. Force a choice between external desire, internal protection, and emerging need.
4. Make consequences persist across later scenes.
5. Prove transformation through changed action under comparable pressure.

Do not declare growth only through introspection or narration. Require transformation evidence in behavior, risk, sacrifice, boundary, or repair.

## Track relationships

- Define what each person wants from the other and what each withholds.
- Track trust, leverage, dependence, intimacy, resentment, and mistaken belief as separate variables when relevant.
- Mark the action or revelation that changes a relationship state.
- Preserve asymmetry: two characters need not interpret the same event alike.
- Let relationship change constrain or enable later choices.

## Evaluate scenes and outlines

- Ask whether the character pursues something rather than merely receives plot events.
- Connect each major turn to a decision, revealed contradiction, tested boundary, or changed relationship.
- Distinguish regression under pressure from erased development.
- Reject convenient behavior that serves the plot but violates established motivation without a shown cause.
- Label proposed psychology as inferred until the author confirms it.
