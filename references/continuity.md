# Continuity Review

## Audit dimensions

Check only against supplied or project-recorded evidence:

- **Chronology:** Verify event order, duration, dates, travel time, and recovery time.
- **Location:** Track who and what can plausibly occupy each place.
- **Physical state:** Carry wounds, fatigue, clothing, appearance, abilities, and environmental effects forward.
- **Inventory:** Track possession, transfer, consumption, damage, concealment, and access.
- **Knowledge state:** Separate what the author, reader, narrator, and each character knows or believes. Require a shown source before a character states, answers, anticipates, or reacts to a concept; a character cannot respond to terminology or information nobody has told or shown them.
- **Relationship state:** Preserve trust, conflict, obligation, status, and the last event that changed them.
- **POV:** Enforce viewpoint access, narrative distance, and established tense or person.
- **Unresolved promises:** Track clues, vows, threats, questions, setups, and expected payoffs.

## Classify findings

- **Critical:** Breaks causal possibility, identity, chronology, mystery fairness, or a confirmed story rule.
- **Major:** Distorts motivation, stakes, relationship logic, viewpoint knowledge, or a promised payoff.
- **Minor:** Creates a local mismatch in description, timing, inventory, wording, or presentation without changing the story engine.
- **Advisory:** Marks ambiguity that may be intentional but needs author confirmation.

Do not treat absence of evidence as a contradiction. Label uncertainty and cite the conflicting passages, records, or scene facts.

## Report before repair

1. Report contradictions before proposing any fix.
2. State severity, conflicting facts, location, and downstream effect.
3. Preserve confirmed canon and identify which fact would need to change.
4. Offer the smallest viable repair plus alternatives when the choice affects intent.
5. Apply no state or manuscript repair until the author selects or confirms it.

After confirmation, propagate the chosen repair through chronology, physical state, inventory, knowledge, relationships, POV, and unresolved promises. Recheck downstream chapters and structured records touched by the change.
