# Revision

## Preserve the source

Keep the original and produce a proposed revision or diff by default. Edit in place only when the author explicitly requests it. Preserve accepted voice, intentional ambiguity, formatting conventions, and the project style guide.

## Revise in strict order

Complete each pass before optimizing the next:

1. **Story structure:** Verify premise delivery, causality, escalation, reversals, climax, and resolution.
2. **Scene purpose:** Require each scene to create a necessary change; cut, combine, or reposition inert scenes.
3. **Character agency:** Make consequential choices arise from established desire, need, contradiction, boundary, background, relationship posture, and pressure. Compare the completed chapter with its chapter character card and the relevant canonical dossier sections from [character-context.md](character-context.md); flag traits listed in the card that never become visible and behavior that has no established cause.
4. **Pacing:** Balance setup, compression, expansion, transition, aftermath, and recovery according to story pressure. Cut structural padding the reader does not need: a chapter opening that recounts the previous chapter's events for readers who already lived them, and manual-style enumerations that list procedures, record fields, form pages, or settings specs without advancing the scene. Keep the minimum setting or procedural detail the scene requires and let it arrive through action; delete the inventory of the rest.
5. **Viewpoint:** Enforce access to perception and knowledge; maintain intended distance, tense, and person.
6. **Dialogue and embodiment:** Give speech intention, resistance, subtext, distinct cadence, oral naturalness, and physical context; apply the ephemeral scene-profile and dialogue tests in [dialogue-and-clarity.md](dialogue-and-clarity.md). Check whether perception, bodily response, practical behavior, and concrete associations arise from the selected dossier background rather than generic reactions.
7. **Imagery:** Select concrete sensory details and motifs that clarify mood, action, setting, or meaning.
8. **Sentence prose:** Improve clarity, rhythm, precision, paragraph flow, and redundancy without flattening voice; replace unsupported compressed judgments, author-level summaries, and unearned maxim-like sentences with concrete evidence or a supported clear summary.
9. **Final continuity:** Recheck chronology, location, physical state, inventory, knowledge, relationships, POV, and unresolved promises; verify the source of every consequential concept a character states or answers.

Do not polish sentences that are likely to be removed by an earlier pass. Do not solve a structural defect through decorative prose.

## Deliver the revision

- State the requested scope and any assumptions.
- Report contradictions before changing them; route the findings through `continuity.md`.
- Separate required corrections from optional craft alternatives.
- Summarize material changes and any unresolved author decisions.
- Propose structured story-state updates separately and merge them only after author confirmation.
