## Optional Eight-Beat Check

> Fill this only when the chapter benefits from a clearer progression chain. Adjacent functions may merge, a function may span chapters, and not-applicable entries may stay blank; these are narrative functions, not eight equal-sized scenes.

- Goal: What does the viewpoint character want now?
- Opportunity: What credible path makes success appear possible?
- Obstacle: What blocks the easy path?
- Response: What action, adaptation, bargain, or refusal follows?
- Disruption: What changes the situation, cost, or plan?
- Reinterpretation: Does the meaning of the situation change? Merge with disruption or leave blank when unnecessary.
- Decision: How does the character answer the new situation? This may be attack, retreat, refusal, sacrifice, compromise, or a mistake.
- New State: What consequence, aftermath, relationship change, pressure, or temporary closure remains?
